The Federal Office of Justice maintains ZEFIX, the swiss commerce register for all legally operating businesses. The register provides us with company name, type, description, and address. ZEFIX is also available as \[Linked Data\](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linked\_data). In this data story, we will show how to work with Linked Data. Mainly, we will see how to work with commerce register data.
