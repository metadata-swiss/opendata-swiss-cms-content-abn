Curated list of all important links around the Open Data offer in Switzerland Awesome OGD Switzerland is a carefully curated list of open government data portals, websites, APIs, tools and other related resources in Switzerland. Relevant links to international sites are also included. The list is updated regularly and is open to additions and suggestions from all interested parties. opendata.swiss and all participating data providers are the focus of Awesome OGD Switzerland.
