Predikon is a website born out of an academic project by the Laboratory for Information and Network Dynamics Laboratory (INDY Lab) at the Swiss Federal Institute for Technology in Lausanne (EPFL). Its main goal is to provide predictions about votes in Switzerland, as soon as municipal results are published by Swiss cantons starting at 12:00pm on the day of a vote. Predikon also provides interactive visualizations of voting behaviors in Switzerland, as learned by our machine-learning algorithm. INDY Lab: https://indy.epfl.ch/; EPFL: https://www.epfl.ch/en/
