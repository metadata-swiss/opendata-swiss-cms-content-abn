The Statistical Office of the Canton of Zurich collects data on the city and its residents. This data is published as \[Linked Data\](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linked\_data). In this tutorial, we will show how to work with Linked Data. Mainly, we will see how to work with real estate dataset.
