ElCom, the Federal Electricity Commission, collects data on the electricity tariffs for household and companies. This data is published as \[Linked Data\](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linked\_data). In this tutorial, we will show how to work with Linked Data. Mainly, we will see how to work with data on electricity tariffs. \_\_Used Datasets\_\_: -API: https://s.zazuko.com/aNmSfW -Metadata: https://energy.ld.admin.ch/elcom/electricityprice
