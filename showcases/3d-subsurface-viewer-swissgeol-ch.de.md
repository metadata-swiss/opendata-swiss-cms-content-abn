Geologic data provides essential information for answering manifold questions in the field of environment, natural resources, energy and waste disposal. It is an important basis for any kind of projects related to infrastructure construction and prevention of natural hazards. swissgeol.ch makes it possible to visualize such 3D geologic data in its correct spatial position in the subsurface and to combine and correlate it with other 2D and 3D data. Various tools enable the users to slice the terrain and get an insight in the subsurface. Note: The list of datasets used is not complete. Only the most important ones have been specified.
